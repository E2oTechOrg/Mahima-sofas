Cancellation Policy

When and how an order with Mahima Sofa's can be cancelled.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Cancelling an order

To cancel, call or message us on +91 96884 47097 as soon as possible and give your name and order details. A cancellation is effective only when we confirm it to you.

Before work starts

If you cancel before we have bought materials or begun making your sofa, we will refund your advance. We may deduct the cost of any work or materials already arranged for your order.

After work starts

Your sofa is made to your own design, size and fabric. Once production has begun it cannot be cancelled, and the advance is not refundable. If you cannot accept delivery, please talk to us and we will try to find a fair solution.

Changes to an order

Changes to the design, size or fabric are possible only before production begins, and may change the price and delivery time. We will confirm any change with you before we act on it.

Cancellation by us

If we are unable to make or deliver your order, we may cancel it. In that case we will refund everything you have paid for that order in full.

Ready-stock items

For sofas we sell from ready stock, please tell us about any cancellation before delivery. After delivery, our Payment & Refund Policy applies.

Questions?

Call or message us and we will be glad to help.
