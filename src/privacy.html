Privacy Policy

How Mahima Sofa's collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who we are

Mahima Sofa's is a sofa manufacturer selling directly from its factory at Chinna Kollapatty, Anna Salai Street, Opp. Mariyamman Kovil, Salem - 636 008, Tamil Nadu. This policy explains how we handle information when you use this website, call us or message us.

Information we collect

When you contact us we may collect your name, phone number, delivery address and the details of the sofa you are asking about. You give us this information yourself, through our contact form, WhatsApp, a phone call or a visit to our showroom.

This website does not have user accounts and we do not ask for card or bank details through it.

How the contact form works

The "Send on WhatsApp" button on this site opens WhatsApp on your device with your message ready to send to our number. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

How we use your information

We use your information only to reply to your enquiries, prepare quotations, make and deliver your order, give after-sales support and keep records of our business. We do not sell your information or use it to send you unrelated promotions.

Sharing

We may share your name, phone number and address with a delivery or transport partner only as needed to deliver your order. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Third-party content

This site shows a Google Map and links to WhatsApp. These services may set cookies or collect technical information under their own policies. We do not control them.

Keeping your information

We keep order and enquiry records for as long as needed to serve you, handle warranty or service requests and meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by calling or messaging us on +91 96884 47097. We will act on your request unless we need to keep the information by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Questions?

Call or message us and we will be glad to help.
